## Overview

THABAT is a smart glove-integrated Mixed Reality (MR) training prototype developed to explore immersive approaches to Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) training.

The system brings together a Unity-based simulation environment, wearable sensing technology, and AI-assisted analysis to support interactive, scenario-based training.

Developed during Defensathon 2026, THABAT demonstrates the potential of combining immersive technologies and intelligent systems in specialized training environments.


## Key Features

- **Immersive MR Training:** Interactive EOD training scenarios developed in Unity.
- **Smart Glove Integration:** Wearable sensing for capturing hand movement and stability data.
- **Real-Time Performance Evaluation:** Rule-based assessment of movement, speed, and hand stability.
- **Color-Coded Monitoring Dashboard:** Visual indicators that reflect changes in hand stability and performance.
- **Progressive Training Levels:** Three scenarios with increasing difficulty and time pressure.
- **Performance-Based Feedback:** Contextual feedback based on accuracy, stability, and task completion.
- **Training Performance Reports:** Summaries of completion time, scores, warnings, and danger events.
